## What the look found

- **Arrests (33 sites on one template).** The "opt-out" page of `ohioarrests.org`, `oklahomaarrests.org`, `scarrests.org` and 30 others is `/corrections` (or `/request-portal`, which
  redirects to it): a "Report an Error" form for the site's own guidance. The page says it cannot change, remove, seal or hide a record, that those belong to the courts, and that
  the way to remove one is expungement. The standard opt-out message would go to an editorial team. The two real sends the classifier called "confirmed" were this form, so they are
  not confirmed opt-outs and were never marked. These sites need a decision: keep them as a low-value channel, or move them to guided with the expungement explanation.
  The other ~84 arrests sites use different forms (many with a Cloudflare check and an "authorized agent" box) and were not re-read this pass.
- **Hand check of 24 sites in people search, `other`, real estate, location data, financial and fraud and identity** (screenshot of each form from the latest dry run): all are real opt-out or
  privacy-request forms, filled with the right details. Things to know: `peoplefindersdaas.com` and `complementics.com` also accept the request by email; `complementics.com`, `veraset.com` and
  `quadrant.io` need the phone's advertising ID, which the app reads only when allowed; `farmers.com` and `usatrace.com` showed only one field (the rest is probably behind a script), so a person
  would fill them; `monitorbase.com` and `fi-navigator.com` carry a bot check.
- **Shared templates.** 35 sites sit on OneTrust privacy-portal forms (28 plus 7 on its CDN), 15 on one people-search network form, 9 on the trustsuperset portal and 33 on the arrests
  "Report an Error" form. A problem with one of these templates (the disabled OneTrust Submit button, the trustsuperset "no success message") is a problem for the whole group, so fixing a
  template is worth more than fixing a site.
