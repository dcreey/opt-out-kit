# Future work: lists and signals each place runs

Researched 2026-10-26 (web search plus the regulators named below). Each item is stored in `jurisdictions/*.yml` under `future_work:` and shown in the app
(Home, for people outside the US, and "Coming later for your state"). `sourced: false` means only the name is known and the details still need checking
against the operator's own site. Nothing here is used by the app yet. This is not legal advice.

The queue still puts a person's own place first (`PlanRank`: official removal service, then our automatic, then semi-automated brokers registered where they
live or in their country, and so on). Outside the US the app has no local registries, so the person is told opt-outs for their country are coming later.

|---|---|---|---|

What the search did not find: any data broker registry outside the US (Canada, Quebec, France, UK and Australia were searched and returned only US results),
and any other state's one-stop deletion mechanism. Claims about broker uptake of DROP (about 30 percent in the first week) are second-hand.
Sources: privacy.ca.gov (DROP), Faegre Drinker and Pandectes (NJ, CT), Wikipedia's Robinson list table, Mondaq and CMS (Turkey), Profeco coverage (Mexico).
