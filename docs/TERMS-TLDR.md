# What the sites' terms say (plain English)

Read 2026-10-21 from the acceptable-use prompt, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy of alabamaarrests.org, usrecords.net and
sdarrests.org (the same network of about 44 "arrests / warrants / records" sites, which share nearly identical text), plus the
notice CourtRecords.us shows. Other sites in a group can differ. This is a summary, not legal advice. The same text is shown in
the app before anyone allows it to accept terms (`App/Sources/TermsSummary.swift`); keep the two in step.

## The arrests, warrants and records network (about 44 sites)

- **Visiting is agreeing.** You are bound just by using the site, and again by tapping "I agree".
- **Lawful use only.** No stalking or harassing anyone, and no false identity to get information.
- **Off limits: public officials, minors and celebrities.** Do not scan or accept for anyone added to Opt-Out Kit who is one of these.
- **18 or older.**
- **Indemnity.** A broad clause makes you hold the site, its advertisers and partners harmless from any claim arising from your use
  or from breaking the terms.
- **You carry the risk.** Records are "as-is" and can be wrong, they are not legal advice, every warranty is disclaimed and liability is limited to nothing.
- **Content.** Do not copy it. Anything you send unasked, they may use and republish.
- **Changes.** They can change the terms at any time without telling you.
- **Money.** Search forms and links earn them commissions, and registering opens a separate third-party paid membership area they do not control. Opt-Out Kit never registers or pays.

## Their privacy policy

- They log visits: IP address, browser, pages, clicks, scrolling and referrer.
- They and their advertisers may set tracking cookies for purchase tracking and ad targeting.
- They say they will not sell or rent information you give them without consent, except for law enforcement, court orders or their own legal defence.
- No security guarantee, no responsibility for breaches, possible storage or transfer abroad.
- They may change the policy without notice; visiting counts as consent.

## CourtRecords.us notice (state court-records sites)

- It is not a consumer reporting agency. You agree not to use results for credit, insurance, employment or tenant decisions.
- It cannot confirm that its information is accurate or complete.
- "I agree" means you accept its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Only a preliminary people search is free; full records need an account or purchase.
- Only this notice was read, not that site's full terms.

## What it means

- Accepting costs no money; none of the prompts and terms read has a fee, subscription or arbitration clause.
- The main commitment is the indemnity clause, which already applies to anyone who visits.
- None of the three terms pages read bans automated access, but the app accepts only when no paragraph does (automation-flagged paragraphs are never accepted).
- Results are unproven, so a listing found there is a lead, not proof.
- **Gap to close:** these terms exclude minors, public officials and celebrities. The app should not scan or accept for a person marked as a minor; a "minor" or "public figure" flag on profiles is not built yet.
