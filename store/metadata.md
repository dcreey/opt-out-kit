**Description**
Hundreds of companies collect and sell your name, address, phone number and more. Exnay helps you take your information back, a little each day.

It keeps a directory of about 830 data brokers, works out the best way to opt out of each one, does as much as it can on your phone, and walks you through the rest.

HOW IT WORKS
• A daily queue. Each day you pick one kind of broker, such as people search, ad tech or recruiting data, and work through a short batch.
• It fills the form for you. Exnay opens the broker's own opt-out page and fills in your details. The app never solves a human check: those, and anything it cannot finish, are left to you.
• Email when that is the way. One opt-out email to the day's sites, sent from your own mail app.
• Email links. When a broker sends a link to confirm, share it to Exnay from your mail app and it opens the form for you.
• Automatic protection (optional). Exnay tries every site it can without you, sends again every 3 months to the ones that went through, and re-tests every site every 6. Whatever does not go through comes to you, with the reason.
• Reminders. Some opt-outs do not last. Exnay brings sites back when it is time to repeat.
• California's DROP. One request that reaches every registered California broker.
• Global Privacy Control. Every page the app opens sends the signal.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS
Each request cites the privacy law that applies to you (CCPA, GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA and more), and each kind of broker has a plain-language briefing, so you learn what the data is used for and what you are entitled to ask for.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No Exnay server, no account, no analytics, no tracking.
• Everything you enter is stored encrypted on your phone.
• Your details go only to the broker you are opting out of.
• Always free. No ads, no subscription.
• Open source: github.com/dcreey/opt-out-kit

**App Review notes**
Exnay has no account or login. Open the app, add a profile (any name and a US address work), then open the Queue tab, pick a kind of broker and tap a site to see the page filled in. Automatic protection is off until the person turns it on at first use; once on, it fills and sends opt-out forms to broker sites itself (a full pass first, a repeat every 3 months and a full re-test every 6) and leaves anything with a human check to the person. The app contacts only the data-broker websites the person opens. The advertising ID is requested (App Tracking Transparency prompt) only when a broker's form asks for it, and is passed only to that form.
